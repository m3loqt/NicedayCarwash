Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Niceday Carwash ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the Niceday Carwash mobile application and booking services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to operate the Service:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and phone number, collected when you register or sign in.
  • Google Sign-In information: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account as provided by Google.
  • Vehicle details: information about the vehicle(s) you book services for.
  • Booking information: your selected branch, service, appointment time, and booking history.
  • Location information: with your permission, your device location is used to show you nearby branches.
  • Profile photo: if you choose to add one, stored to personalize your account.
  • Push notification data: a device token used to send you booking status updates and reminders.
  • Payment information: booking fees are processed through our third party payment partner, Maya. We do not collect or store your full card or payment credentials ourselves.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process and manage your bookings and payments
  • Send you booking confirmations, status updates, and appointment reminders
  • Show you nearby branches based on your location
  • Respond to your questions and provide customer support
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of the Service

3. How We Share Your Information

We share limited information with branch staff (such as your name, phone number, vehicle details, and booking information) so they can prepare for and fulfill your appointment. We share payment information with our payment processor, Maya, solely to process your booking fee. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and booking information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or ask us to stop using it, by contacting us using the details below. You can also update most of your account information directly within the app under your profile settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for users who are at least 18 years old, or who have the consent of a parent or legal guardian, consistent with our Terms and Conditions. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without such consent.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us through the details available in the app, or visit nicedaycarwash.ph.
